Spotting scams and staying compliant

A legitimate provider never charges illegal upfront fees and always puts terms in writing before you commit. If someone guarantees they can erase your debt overnight, pressures you to pay before doing anything, or asks you to stop opening mail from a court, treat it as a red flag. Our Scams & Compliance guide and Scam Warning walk through the warning signs so you can protect yourself and verify anyone you consider working with.
